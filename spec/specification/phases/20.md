# Phase 20 — Hosting provider, reseller and customer management

**Owner role**  Hosting product lead  
**Documentation status**  Drafted and structurally checked  
**Engineering status**  Proposed, with runtime evidence pending

## Purpose and scope

Separate provider, reseller and agency relationships from identity scopes. Model customer, account, plan version, upstream allocation, reserved usage, site membership and support lease independently. Serialize allocation updates and enforce downstream ceilings against upstream entitlement. Support handover and customer deletion with retention. Treat billing, payments, portals and SLA modules as candidates with individual scope gates.

## Contract and operational behavior

Provider allocates capacity to reseller; reseller suballocates from ceilings; agency operates sites through grants. Customer contacts do not automatically become panel principals. Account units track reservations, consumption, limits and plan version. Updating a plan creates a new version and migration intent. Support lease terms include approver, purpose, actions, expiry and revoke. Optional commerce uses audited provider reconciliation and append-only ledger events.

## Acceptance and test design

**REQ-P20-01 / AC-P20-01**. Concurrent allocation cannot consume more than remaining capacity. A revoked upstream or expired support grant cannot reach customer data. Plan changes preserve versioned historical entitlements.

**TC-P20-01 · Procedure**. Race two reservations of 6 against available 10, then attempt support access after lease expiration.

**Expected**. One reservation commits, the other receives conflict. Expired support cannot access data and is audited.

**TC-P20-02 · Companion test**. Create an account on versioned plan within its parent entitlement.

**Expected**. One live account consumes its reservation and records the effective plan version.
