# Phase 16 — Email and external service integrations

**Owner role**  Integrations lead  
**Documentation status**  Drafted and structurally checked  
**Engineering status**  Proposed, with runtime evidence pending

## Purpose and scope

Mail for recovery uses configured SMTP with transport/security checks, queued delivery and redacted diagnostics. Provider credentials and webhook keys use rotation and narrow roles. Optional mailbox hosting, aliases, forwarding, deliverability and webmail remain distinct capabilities. Separate provider acceptance from delivery evidence. If the selected adapter lacks trusted delivery evidence, delivery remains unknown. Suppress duplicate sends where the provider supports deduplication and surface uncertainty otherwise.

## Contract and operational behavior

Define adapter classes for transactional SMTP, optional mailbox, external identity and event integrations. SMTP record holds endpoint, TLS policy, credential reference and status. Verify provider failures as transient/permanent and distinguish accepted from delivered. Avoid cross-user inference from reset responses. Callback receiver resolves tenant from an authenticated integration binding. Outage leaves reset requests generic, dashboards usable and operator diagnostics scoped.

## Acceptance and test design

**REQ-P16-01 / AC-P16-01**. Unauthenticated reset requests produce the same response for known and unknown accounts. Permanent provider failure stops retrying. Logs cannot expose addresses beyond policy, reset links or transport secrets.

**TC-P16-01 · Procedure**. Queue reset mail against a provider that emits temporary failure then permanent rejection. Compare known and unknown account responses.

**Expected**. Public responses identical by contract. Retry schedule respected, terminal failure recorded and no reset material leaked.

**TC-P16-02 · Companion test**. Send transactional mail through the authorized test provider and collect delivery event.

**Expected**. Accepted and delivered observations are recorded separately; redaction holds.

## v2.0 audit correction

**AUD-16 · High.** Delivery was treated as observable across all mail providers. Optional mailbox hosting and transactional SMTP are different scopes.

SMTP acceptance is observable. Delivery requires trusted separate evidence, and otherwise remains unknown. Deduplicate provider webhooks and avoid retrying ambiguous sends without provider-supported reconciliation.

**Blocking decisions**  DEC-007,DEC-011. Review the [decision log](../registers/decisions.csv) before freezing the affected implementation contract.
