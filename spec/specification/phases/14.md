# Phase 14 — Backups, restore and disaster recovery

**Owner role**  Reliability lead  
**Documentation status**  Drafted and structurally checked  
**Engineering status**  Proposed, with runtime evidence pending

## Purpose and scope

Capture data, configuration and required metadata with consistency markers. Encrypt backups with independently recoverable keys, record digests and store an off-host copy when policy requires. Restore to an isolated target first, verify records and health, then cut over with rollback access. Enforce retention, holds and deletion audit. Measure RPO from latest recovered data timestamp and RTO from incident declaration to successful verification.

## Contract and operational behavior

Backup manifest covers schema/application version, consistency timestamp, object checksums, size, storage destination, encryption key ID, and ownership. Lifecycle is requested → capturing → verified → available → expired/deleted. Failed verification quarantines artifact. Restore binds destination, compatibility and selected point, records integrity checks and creates a recovery operation. Exercise independent key access and off-host recovery after total VPS loss where committed. Record bytes restored, recovered timestamp, incident start and verified service time.

## Acceptance and test design

**REQ-P14-01 / AC-P14-01**. Failed integrity or missing decryption material blocks cutover. A successful restore has checksum and functional evidence, explicit recovery point and measured duration.

**TC-P14-01 · Procedure**. Restore a tampered archive and a good archive into an isolated environment. Compare seeded record IDs, digests and health.

**Expected**. Tampered archive cannot activate. Good archive matches inventory and produces RPO/RTO measurements.

**TC-P14-02 · Companion test**. Capture a known recovery point and restore to an isolated target using the approved key.

**Expected**. Restored records and health match fixture, and RPO/RTO measurements are attached.
