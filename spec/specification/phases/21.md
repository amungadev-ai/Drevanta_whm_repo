# Phase 21 — Monitoring, logging, alerts and audit trails

**Owner role**  Observability lead  
**Documentation status**  Drafted and structurally checked  
**Engineering status**  Proposed, with runtime evidence pending

## Purpose and scope

Differentiate metric samples, health observations, operational logs, alert incidents and immutable audit facts. Add actor/subject and outcome to audit, operation/request correlation across boundaries, and observed timestamp/freshness to health. Apply tenant filters and redaction before export. De-duplicate alerts by scope, rule and incident state. Stale observations surface unknown.

## Contract and operational behavior

Audit schema includes event ID, UTC occurred time, actor, scope, action, resource, outcome, correlation, operation ID and safe reason. Metrics carry sample time, freshness and bounded labels. Logs carry level and scrubbed structured context. Alert rules bind scope, window, severity, dedup key and routing. Export requires explicit access check at request and retrieval. Operational logs cannot replace security audit evidence. Status pages have an independent exposure policy.

## Acceptance and test design

**REQ-P21-01 / AC-P21-01**. A missing heartbeat transitions to unknown after the configured threshold. A tenant cannot read another tenant's telemetry and secret canaries never appear in logs or exports.

**TC-P21-01 · Procedure**. Stop heartbeat ingestion, replay alert samples, and generate messages with credential markers from two tenants.

**Expected**. Health becomes unknown, one incident remains open, exports scoped and zero credential canaries appear.

**TC-P21-02 · Companion test**. Emit one alert-triggering observation and resolve after healthy sample.

**Expected**. Exactly one scoped incident opens and resolves with traceable sample and notification.
