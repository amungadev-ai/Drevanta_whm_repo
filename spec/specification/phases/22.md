# Phase 22 — Background jobs, automation and notifications

**Owner role**  Orchestration lead  
**Documentation status**  Drafted and structurally checked  
**Engineering status**  Proposed, with runtime evidence pending

## Purpose and scope

Persist jobs, attempts, leases, fences, checkpoints and outbox. Recheck current scope and generations before execution. Bound retries to transient classes. Recover unknown outcomes by observation. Cancel before irreversible effects or mark cancellation requested and reconcile. Schedules record timezone, next due time, missed-run policy and concurrency. Notification projection has recipient-specific delivery state.

## Contract and operational behavior

Operation states: queued → running → succeeded/failed, with waiting_retry, retry/reconciling, and cancellation_requested/canceled branches. Lease and fencing guard effects and completion. Terminal success requires observed postcondition. Queue consumers process at least-once; outbox projections deduplicate by event ID. Decide retry counts and backoff, deadline, reconciliation cadence, dead-letter handling and worker leases in DEC-009. For schedules persist local timezone plus UTC occurrence identity, with explicit DST ambiguity and missed-run policy.

## Acceptance and test design

**REQ-P22-01 / AC-P22-01**. Expired lease allows a new attempt while fencing the old one. A repeated trigger has one logical job per scope and schedule occurrence. Ambiguous effects never retry blindly.

**TC-P22-01 · Procedure**. Pause worker after host effect, expire lease, run a successor, then resume stale worker and replay trigger.

**Expected**. Successor observes existing effect. Stale worker cannot commit. Exactly one logical job and no duplicate effect.

**TC-P22-02 · Companion test**. Schedule one occurrence and run a successful worker with valid lease.

**Expected**. One operation succeeds after verified effect and one recipient gets the expected event.
